Skyknow Checkout
PayPal Live · D1 Orders · Resend Delivery
Owned checkout infrastructure

Payment and download delivery for validated Skyknow products.

This is the Skyknow payment layer: PayPal creates and captures orders, Cloudflare signs expiring download links, D1 records completed purchases, and Resend sends delivery email.

This page is now intentionally branded as infrastructure, not as a random product storefront. Products should only be promoted after QA and market validation.

1. Pay

Server-side PayPal Live order creation. Secrets are kept in Cloudflare Pages, not the browser.

2. Deliver

Completed orders receive signed, expiring download links; product files stay private.

3. Recover

Buyer can regenerate a download link with PayPal order ID and email.
